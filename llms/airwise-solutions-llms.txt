# AirWise Solutions

> AirWise is a unified drone operating software platform for public safety and critical infrastructure teams. It covers the full mission lifecycle: pre-flight planning and FAA authorizations (including LAANC), real-time in-flight airspace awareness, and post-flight data, analytics, and compliance. The platform is hardware-agnostic, UTM-ready, and built for beyond-visual-line-of-sight (BVLOS) operations.

> AirWise Solutions is headquartered in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and was founded in 2021. The platform fuses ADS-B, Remote ID, RF, radar, and weather data into a single operating picture. It integrates with Skydio, DJI, and Autel aircraft and connects to enterprise systems through open APIs. Customers include public safety agencies, energy companies, and infrastructure operators, among them the Washington State Patrol. The most important pages are listed below.
